# Printed parts

Print one of each of the 35 numbered STLs. They are millimetre geometry, translated individually to minimum X/Y/Z zero. Translation does not choose the strongest or most support-efficient orientation. Check walls, overhangs, captive-nut insertion, lettering, tolerances, hole access and brim/support requirements in the slicer. A 220 x 220 mm usable bed is assumed; the largest nominal XY footprint is 165 x 190 mm before slicer additions.

The six acrylic rear panels are not included here. Use ../cnc for cutting files and ../exports for the corresponding editable solids. Use a small fastener/splice/retainer trial before the full print run. Verify actual panel/supply/gland dimensions first. No physical print, temperature or load test has been completed.
